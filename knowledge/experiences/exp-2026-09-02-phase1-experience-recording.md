## Findings
1. The SinusTDD distinction between product evidence and infrastructure failure was immediately useful: a zero-step Actions failure must not be mislabeled as RED.
2. Preview/write separation fits WikiSkill naturally and gives MCP honest effect annotations.
3. Experience persistence can remain ordinary OKF Markdown: no JSON ledger or parallel database is needed.
4. A write should validate the whole bundle, remove the candidate on failure, and refresh the in-memory bundle only after a conformant result.
5. Full repository CI remains an external validation gap until GitHub runner allocation recovers, so this Experience is `partial` rather than `success`.
